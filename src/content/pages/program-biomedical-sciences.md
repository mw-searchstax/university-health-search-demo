## Models, images and labels

The Biomedical Sciences program at fictional Alder Coast University brings several kinds of teaching object into one catalogue. A model entry records the object name, the labels visible on its base and the course collection where it is stored. Image entries use invented filenames and neutral descriptions, allowing learners to compare catalogue fields without relying on clinical images or research results.

## Laboratory notation

Students encounter sample laboratory labels, diagram keys and fictional image captions as examples of how scientific material is indexed. One exercise compares a short caption with a longer catalogue note and asks which terms identify the subject, method or format. The examples demonstrate classification choices rather than laboratory procedures.

The program connects work in anatomy and neuroscience with the institution's imaging and laboratory-services vocabulary. This overview describes an academic collection and its teaching structure. It does not report discoveries or provide claims about qualifications or outcomes.
