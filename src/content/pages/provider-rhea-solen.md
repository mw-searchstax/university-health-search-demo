## Mock referral narratives

Rhea Solen curates mock referral narratives for Primary Care seminars. The examples show how a fictional document can identify its sender, intended department and attached materials. Blank templates let learners examine document roles and handoffs without completing a form or directing a real referral.

## Seminar setting

The narrative set is kept in the Teaching Health Center’s interdisciplinary classroom. Solen groups examples by the document’s purpose and annotates where the fictional handoff occurs. The materials are not live referrals and contain no real names or contact details. Their purpose is to support discussion of record structure and departmental coordination. This page does not explain how to obtain care or promise that any service is available.
