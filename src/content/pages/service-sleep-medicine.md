## Three areas of focus

Alder Coast's Sleep Medicine unit brings together sleep-related breathing, difficulty sleeping and the timing of sleep within Pulmonary and Sleep Services. Its subject areas include:

- Sleep apnea, in which breathing repeatedly stops and restarts during sleep.
- Insomnia, which can involve difficulty falling asleep, staying asleep or getting good-quality sleep.
- Circadian rhythms, the body's repeating daily cycles that help govern sleep and waking.

## Department and center

The unit is based at the fictional Alder Coast Sleep Center. The department defines the academic subject area; the center provides the campus setting, with demonstration bedrooms and an adjoining observation room.

Faculty use room plans, sample recordings and equipment displays to introduce learners to this setting. Mara Ellison's profile describes one faculty role in that work. This site does not offer assessments or overnight studies.
