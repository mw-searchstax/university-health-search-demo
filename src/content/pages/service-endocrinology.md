## Endocrine-system topics

The fictional Endocrinology department groups classroom materials about the endocrine system. Labeled gland models are paired with terminology cards and illustrated diagrams. Each card identifies the subject shown and points to the matching object in the teaching collection.

## Materials in the South Campus annex

The models and cards are kept in the South Campus Clinic’s academic rooms. A related provider profile describes the labels and inventory conventions used there. Other departments share the annex, so the collection index records which materials belong to Endocrinology. This overview explains a teaching collection and its organization. It does not interpret test values or provide guidance about health conditions.
