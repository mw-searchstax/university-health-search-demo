## Information in public settings

Community Screening maintains a reading collection that distinguishes vocabulary cards, sample leaflets and dated institutional notices. Health Literacy examines how plain-language labels, large-print formats and glossary layouts affect the way educational material is organized.

Transit Access explores map symbols and the difference between pedestrian routes, covered links and transit concepts on an invented campus. It provides no real routes or schedules.

## University connections

Public Health Partnerships maps responsibilities between fictional university departments. School Health Education organizes reading kits and teacher-facing topic cards, with links to the child-health teaching collection.

These program descriptions continue across individual academic years. Historical announcements about rooms, catalogues and displays belong in the news archive. No program offers tests, registration or services through this demonstration website.
