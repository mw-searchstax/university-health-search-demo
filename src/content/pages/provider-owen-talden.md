## Glossary navigation cards

Owen Talden develops navigation cards for the fictional Oncology reading collection. Each card points to a shelf section or a general vocabulary page and records the terms used in the collection’s catalog. The cards help students move among institutional reading materials without suggesting that separate conditions or topics are equivalent.

## Reading collection at the hospital

The cards sit in the university hospital’s academic wing with a subject index and blank catalogue slips. Talden maintains the labels and checks that the index reflects the collection’s organization. This profile describes an educational library resource related to cancer as an academic subject. It reports no research findings, clinical outcomes or promises, and does not guide personal medical decisions.
