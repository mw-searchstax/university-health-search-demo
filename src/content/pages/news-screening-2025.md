## Catalogue sections reorganized in 2025

On April 12, 2025, fictional Alder Coast University reorganized the Community Screening catalogue into three sections: vocabulary cards, sample leaflets and dated university notices. The change gave each document type a clearly labelled shelf and index entry. It followed the earlier library-room reassignment but described a catalogue structure, not a new event or a change in health services.

## What the notice recorded

The vocabulary section holds reference terms; the leaflet section groups sample program descriptions; and the archive section keeps dated institutional notices in chronological order. This distinction helps a reader tell an enduring program description from a record of a particular university decision.

This historical community screening notice records an administrative reorganization completed in 2025. The date identifies when the catalogue update was published. The item describes no screening activity, results or future plans.
