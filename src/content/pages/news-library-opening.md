## What moved into the reading room

The new room brings the anatomy books, diagrams, and model catalogs into one place. A table beside the shelves gives students space to lay out an illustration and its catalog entry together.

The Biomedical Sciences teaching collection supplied the opening display. The wall index shows where to find the books and diagrams that go with each model in the display.
