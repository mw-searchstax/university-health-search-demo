## A new reading room

On June 9, 2026, Alder Coast University opened a fictional teaching-library room beside its university hospital campus. The room brought illustrated anatomy books, diagram folders and model catalogues into a shared reading area. Shelves were arranged by material type, with a table for comparing a printed illustration to its catalogue description.

The arrangement was designed for academic reading and classroom reference.
