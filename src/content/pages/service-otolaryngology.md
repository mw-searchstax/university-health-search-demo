## Ear, nose and throat

Otolaryngology is a medical specialty concerned with the ears, nose, throat and related parts of the head and neck. At fictional Central County University Health, this department overview introduces that subject area and helps visitors recognize its formal name in the care directory.

## Topics within the specialty

The department's subject areas include:

- Ears and hearing.
- The nose and sinuses.
- The throat and voice.
