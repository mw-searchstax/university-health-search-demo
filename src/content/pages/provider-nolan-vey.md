## Gland models and term cards

Nolan Vey prepares labeled gland models and terminology cards for Endocrinology classes. Each card pairs a model name with a neutral caption that identifies the parts shown in the classroom display. The inventory records the model’s location and the matching illustration, creating a simple guide to the collection’s organization.

## South Campus display

At the South Campus Clinic’s academic annex, the models are arranged alongside reference diagrams and blank worksheets. Vey maintains the labels and makes sure that each classroom object can be matched to its caption. The Endocrinology materials introduce the endocrine system as a subject area without interpreting test values or discussing an individual’s health. This profile is about the fictional teaching collection and its catalog, not clinical recommendations.
