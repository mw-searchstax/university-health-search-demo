## Foundations and case discussion

The Medicine curriculum at fictional Alder Coast University organizes foundational subjects around small-group discussion and a shared collection of teaching materials. Sample case cards describe invented settings and questions for classroom analysis. They are designed to help learners distinguish a recorded detail from an interpretation, with no real patient histories represented.

## Anatomy collection

The teaching collection includes labelled models, diagram sets and a catalogue of anatomical terms. Instructors use the catalogue to connect classroom sessions with the rooms at the Teaching Health Center. The materials are grouped by body system and by format, so a learner can move from a model label to a diagram key or a short glossary entry.

Primary Care appears in the curriculum as an academic subject area. This page describes how a fictional program arranges its teaching resources; it does not describe credentials, admissions decisions or clinical services.
