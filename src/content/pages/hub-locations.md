## An invented campus

University Hospital is the campus anchor, with teaching wings and a connection to the library. North Campus Clinic and South Campus Clinic are smaller academic annexes with shared classrooms and departmental collections.

The Heart Center holds an anatomy gallery; the Sleep Center includes demonstration bedrooms and an observation room. Rehabilitation Center uses a mock apartment and adaptable furniture displays. Children's Clinic houses child-health teaching models and a picture-book alcove.

## Shared settings

Teaching Health Center brings together simulation rooms and invented case discussions. Community Health Pavilion has a map table and public-information reading materials. Each profile distinguishes a building's layout and purpose from the subjects of the departments that use it.

These are fictional locations. The profiles contain no real addresses, transport schedules, visiting hours or clinical access arrangements.
