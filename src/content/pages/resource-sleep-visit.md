## Orientation materials

The fictional Sleep Center keeps an orientation packet with a floor-plan key, room labels and a document index. The key groups spaces by their use in the campus demonstration, while the index distinguishes a room overview from equipment descriptions and other reference sheets. These labels explain the arrangement of the collection rather than how to prepare for a study.

## A campus setting

The packet sits with Sleep Medicine teaching materials and a profile of the center’s demonstration rooms. The location page describes the building; this resource focuses on how its printed orientation documents are labeled. All rooms and materials are invented. This page does not promise studies or provide preparation instructions.
