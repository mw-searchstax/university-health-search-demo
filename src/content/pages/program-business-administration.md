## Case-based study

The fictional Master of Business Administration curriculum uses organizational case studies to introduce budgeting, operations planning and decision-making. A sample case follows how a campus unit might organize a small materials collection, from estimating costs to reviewing the steps in its daily workflow.

## Sample learning materials

A budgeting worksheet, an operations-planning workshop and an illustrative course-sequence chart show how the topics connect. These materials describe an academic example only; they make no claim about current admissions, application deadlines or an active enrollment period.
