## Anatomy atlas

Jonah Fenwick maintains a teaching atlas for the fictional Cardiology department. A companion index groups the diagrams by structure, so learners can compare how one subject appears across several teaching plates.

## At the Heart Center

The atlas is stored with the Heart Center’s anatomy collection. Fenwick’s role in this demonstration is editorial: organizing labels, captions and references for classroom use. The collection also includes an annotated wall diagram and a set of blank worksheet layouts. Cardiology appears here as the academic department associated with those materials; the page makes no claims about clinical care or professional credentials.
