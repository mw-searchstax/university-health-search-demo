## Inside the building

The seminar rooms are shared by Dermatology, Endocrinology, and Women’s Health. Each department has a separate display. The room index groups the displays by department so students can find the collection their seminar is using.

## Building use

The departments use the rooms for small-group teaching. This fictional location offers no clinical services or appointments.
