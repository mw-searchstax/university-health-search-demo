## Room layouts and recording charts

Dorian Weller catalogs demonstration-room layouts and fictional recording charts for Pulmonary and Sleep Services teaching. The room plans identify equipment displays, storage areas and the path between adjoining teaching spaces. A separate chart index records the title and format of each blank example, making it easy to distinguish the paper materials from the room diagrams.

## Sleep Center collection

The collection is kept at the fictional Sleep Center with other orientation materials. Weller maintains the labels and files the plans by room, while the charts are grouped by their classroom purpose. These are display and teaching props, not clinical records or instructions for conducting a study. The linked Sleep Medicine service page describes a separate subject overview; this profile focuses on the campus collection and how it is arranged.
