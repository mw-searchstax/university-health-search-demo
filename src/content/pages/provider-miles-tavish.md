## Observation exercise cards

Miles Tavish develops illustrated exercises for Neurology classes. Each card presents a short, invented scenario and asks learners to separate what the fictional observer records from what the observer assumes. A companion set of drawings gives the class a consistent visual reference while students discuss how descriptions are organized.

## North Campus materials

The exercise cards are stored in a shared classroom at the North Campus Clinic’s academic annex. Tavish keeps the scenario sheets with blank observation tables and a catalog of the illustrations. The cases are synthetic and do not model an actual person or provide diagnostic guidance. This collection complements the nerve-pathway atlas at the university hospital, but focuses on written observation rather than anatomical labels.
