## Laboratory orientation materials

Felix Arden maintains simulated specimen labels and blank inventory forms for Laboratory Services orientation. Each label uses an invented identifier and a category name from the classroom collection. The accompanying inventory sheet shows how the teaching set is arranged in storage, allowing students to compare a specimen label with its catalog entry.

## A fictional collection

The materials are kept in the university hospital’s laboratory teaching area. Arden updates the blank forms when a storage tray or display category changes, while preserving the same fictional identifiers across the set. No real samples, people or test results are represented. The page covers laboratory organization as an academic topic and does not interpret results or tell readers how to prepare for a test.
