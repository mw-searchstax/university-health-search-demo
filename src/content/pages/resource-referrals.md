## Document roles in a sample referral

This fictional referral example separates a cover note, a subject summary and an attachment list. Labels identify who the invented document is for within the university’s sample collection and what supporting material accompanies it. The sample illustrates document structure without providing a form to complete.

## Primary Care collection

The example is filed with Primary Care teaching materials and an appointment-packet overview. Its purpose is to show how a document can identify a handoff and list related pages. Names, institutions and details are invented; no real person or destination is represented. This resource does not advise readers how to obtain care or arrange a referral.
