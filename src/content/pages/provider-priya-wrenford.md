## Seminar transcript set

Priya Wrenford organizes fictional conversation transcripts for Behavioral Health seminars. The set uses invented speakers and situations to examine how a record distinguishes a direct quotation, a paraphrase and an observer’s description. A guide notes where attribution appears and how a change in wording can alter the tone of a sample passage.

## Teaching Health Center collection

The transcripts are filed with blank annotation sheets in the Teaching Health Center’s classroom library. Wrenford keeps the examples grouped by documentation topic rather than by person or diagnosis. The materials support discussion of language and respectful documentation; they are not records of real conversations and do not offer counseling or guidance for individual circumstances. The linked service page introduces the department’s academic scope.
