## A directory of reference materials

General Health Services is a fictional directory for organizing primary-care reference material, campus health notices and examples of institutional documents. Its sections distinguish a department overview from a general reference guide and from a sample notice.

## Comparing document types

For example, a flu shots notice has its own session label and edition details, while a department overview describes how a collection is arranged. The directory is an editorial example and does not describe service availability or provide clinical advice.
