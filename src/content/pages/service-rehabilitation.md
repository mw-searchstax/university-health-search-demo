## Function and environment

The fictional Rehabilitation service line introduces teaching topics through the design of functional environments. Its classroom collection pairs mock apartment layouts with adjustable furniture, annotated plans and blank design grids. These objects help explain how the institution organizes learning around rooms and their features.

## At the Rehabilitation Center

The center displays the plans and furniture together so visitors can compare a drawing with the item it represents. Related profiles describe the layout collection and the adjustable furniture display. Accessibility materials elsewhere in the site discuss formats and wayfinding conventions. This department page focuses on educational resources and campus collections. It does not evaluate a person’s abilities or recommend changes to a living space.
