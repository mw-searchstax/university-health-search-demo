## Shared simulation setting

The Teaching Health Center is a fictional campus setting with rooms for invented case discussions, blank charts and interdisciplinary classroom work. A room index groups the materials by seminar and shows where the sample documents are stored. The charts and scenarios contain no real personal information.

## A shared learning environment

Primary Care and Behavioral Health materials are both represented in the center. Provider profiles describe the case cards, mock referrals and fictional transcript set used in the rooms. The location entry provides the common setting for those collections without presenting an operating facility. It includes no address, appointment information or contact channel, and no form for submitting information.
