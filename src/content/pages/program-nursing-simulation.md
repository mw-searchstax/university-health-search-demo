## Planning a classroom exercise

Nursing Simulation is a fictional course about preparing and documenting a classroom exercise. Its sample materials pair an invented room plan with a prop checklist and a blank observation log, showing how documents can be grouped before and after a teaching session.

## Materials in context

The exercise focuses on the organization of classroom materials and written observations. Enrollment status is fictional demonstration data; the exercise includes no patient scenario or clinical instructions and makes no claim of clinical competence.
