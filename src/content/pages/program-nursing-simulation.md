## Before the exercise

Students work in pairs. Each pair uses a floor plan and an equipment checklist to lay out the practice room. They agree on where the observer will sit and what the observation log should capture.

## After the exercise

Pairs compare their logs against the same exercise. If one person recorded a detail the other missed, they discuss whether the room setup affected what each person could see. The group uses those notes to revise the floor plan for the next session.
