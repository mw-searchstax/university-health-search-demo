## Changes recorded over time

The Community Screening notices follow three different administrative changes. In 2024, a library room was reassigned to the reference collection. The 2025 update concerns its catalogue; the 2026 update records a reading-copy display at the Community Health Pavilion.

Two other notices describe the opening of a teaching-library reading room and a past Nursing Open Day display. They document changes to fictional university spaces and materials rather than announcing current events.

## Reading an archived notice

Publication dates identify when each institutional update was issued. They are separate from any later editorial update and from the time a search crawler visits a page. The archive lists the most recently published notices first.

All events and institutional details are invented. No notice provides registration, clinical results, a screening schedule or an invitation to seek care.
