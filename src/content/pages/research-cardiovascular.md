## Illustration archive

The Cardiovascular Research Center at fictional Alder Coast University maintains a catalogue of anatomy illustrations, diagram keys and model photographs created for teaching and discussion. Each entry records a subject label, the format of the item and the collection that holds it. The archive makes it possible to compare how one topic is represented across a labelled model, a flat illustration and a seminar handout.

## Questions and cataloguing

Researchers use a question index to organize hypothetical topics for future reading and seminar discussion. The index sorts entries by anatomy, circulation, rhythm and research method. These are organizing labels only; this page does not describe an active study, present findings or report clinical outcomes.

The center shares vocabulary with the Cardiology department and the residency's academic materials, while retaining a distinct role as a research-oriented catalogue. Its collection focuses on how illustrations and questions are described, not on treatment or patient services.
