## Faculty role

Mara Ellison develops equipment demonstrations at fictional Alder Coast University Health. Her teaching materials include detached sensor leads, blank recording charts and illustrated room plans. These props give learners a way to recognize the parts of a demonstration setup without collecting information from a real person.

## Work at the campus center

Ellison maintains the orientation room's equipment cabinet and a set of illustrated case cards. Each card identifies a piece of equipment, its place in the room and the vocabulary used to describe it. The examples use invented scenarios and contain no patient records.

Her work connects the department's classes with the physical setting shown in the location profile. The equipment displays are teaching props; this profile does not provide instructions for carrying out a test or interpreting a clinical result.
