## Nerve-pathway atlas

Lena Calder organizes an illustrated atlas used in Neurology teaching sessions. Each sheet pairs a simplified pathway drawing with a caption describing the labels and orientation used in the lesson. An inventory records which model accompanies each sheet, including reusable diagrams, a tabletop model and large-format wall illustrations.

## Collection in context

The fictional university hospital keeps the atlas near its teaching rooms, where Neurology materials can be compared with broader Biomedical Sciences references. Calder’s page describes a collection and its cataloguing conventions, not an individual care role. Captions are designed to help learners follow the layout of the diagrams without offering interpretation of symptoms, tests or health conditions.
