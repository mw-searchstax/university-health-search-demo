## A gallery orientation packet

Visiting the Heart Center introduces the fictional Cardiology gallery through a room index, model labels and a short guide to the anatomy classroom. Each document has a distinct role: the index names the spaces, the labels identify the teaching models, and the guide connects the two parts of the collection.

## Heart Center materials

The packet is designed for the university’s sample document library and pairs with the Heart Center location profile. A related department page describes Cardiology’s broader academic collection, while provider profiles focus on individual diagram sets. This resource describes an invented campus exhibit and its paperwork. It contains no directions, visiting hours, contact details or clinical information.
