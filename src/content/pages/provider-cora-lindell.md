## Synthetic image sets

Cora Lindell explains how the fictional Imaging unit labels and catalogues synthetic image sets. Each set has an invented collection code, a caption describing the diagram or image type, and a note that identifies its place in the classroom sequence. A separate index keeps teaching images distinct from records that might be associated with actual care.

## Cataloguing at the hospital

The image index is stored in the university hospital’s academic wing with sample labels and blank catalogue cards. Lindell’s role is to maintain those labels and make the collection’s fictional status visible. The examples contain no patient images or personal information. This profile concerns the organization of educational materials; it does not explain diagnosis or imply that the images are used to make clinical decisions.
