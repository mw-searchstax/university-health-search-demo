## Continuity case cards

Niko Maren develops a set of fictional continuity case cards for Primary Care seminars. Each card separates a stated observation from an assumption and leaves space for learners to discuss how the two differ in a written record. The cases are invented teaching materials; they do not describe real people or provide clinical guidance.

## North Campus classroom set

At the North Campus Clinic’s academic annex, the cards are stored with a timeline board and blank note templates. Maren maintains the sequence and labels so instructors can compare how a scenario changes when new fictional details are introduced. The Primary Care collection emphasizes broad, connected subject areas rather than one specialist topic. This page covers classroom materials and their organization, not services or instructions for obtaining care.
