## Rotation map and teaching objects

The Cardiology Residency page describes a fictional academic rotation map used to organize seminars and shared learning materials at Alder Coast University Health. Its first section groups labelled heart models with anatomy diagrams. A case-library section holds invented discussion cards and blank note templates; the cards are teaching objects, not records of actual care.

## Seminar catalogue

The seminar catalogue connects the curriculum to cardiovascular research topics and the Heart and Vascular service line's subject vocabulary. A sample schedule in the classroom collection is organized by theme—anatomy, rhythm, circulation and research methods—rather than by patient activity. Faculty notes identify which collection supports each discussion and where the corresponding models are displayed.

This page is an academic description of a fictional residency's materials and organization. It does not make accreditation, eligibility or professional-qualification claims, and it does not offer clinical services.
