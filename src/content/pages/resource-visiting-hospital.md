## Reading a floor-plan legend

The fictional hospital floor-plan legend uses colors and symbols to label building zones. A key names the teaching wing, library connection, seminar rooms and exhibit spaces shown on the diagram. The legend is a sample institutional artifact intended to explain the map’s labels.

## Building zones and collections

Related department pages use the academic wing as a setting for their teaching collections. This resource focuses on the meaning of the diagram’s symbols and the way zones are grouped in the university archive. It does not provide directions, visiting hours, permission requirements or information about actual facilities. The hospital and map are invented for this demonstration.
