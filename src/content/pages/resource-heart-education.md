## Everyday and medical terms

“Heart attack” is the everyday term for myocardial infarction, according to the National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute. The institute describes it as a sudden blockage of blood flow carrying oxygen to part of the heart muscle. This short definition is included as neutral vocabulary for the fictional Cardiology teaching collection.

## A glossary entry

The Heart Center’s classroom materials place this term beside labeled heart diagrams and a cross-reference card for myocardial infarction. The card connects everyday wording with the medical term used in the collection. This page is a terminology resource, not a symptom checklist or an action guide. Source: National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute, “Heart Attack,” National Institutes of Health.
