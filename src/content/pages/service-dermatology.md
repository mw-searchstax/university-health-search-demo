## Skin illustrations and captions

The fictional Dermatology department maintains schematic illustrations for classroom discussion of skin as an academic subject. Captions identify what each drawing depicts, and a provenance card keeps the fictional collection number with its image. The materials are organized as diagrams rather than patient photographs.

## Shared teaching annex

The collection is displayed in the South Campus Clinic’s teaching annex, where seminar rooms also hold materials from other departments. A related profile describes the illustration captions and cataloguing practice. The department page explains the subject area and the way its visual collection is arranged. It does not interpret skin changes, offer advice or describe the availability of care.
