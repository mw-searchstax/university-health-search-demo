## Reading catalogue and illustration register

Soren Hale maintains an annotated reading catalogue for the fictional Women’s Health collection. Entries describe the subject and format of each classroom text, while an illustration register identifies the diagrams and captions stored alongside it. Together, the lists show how reading materials and anatomy illustrations are cross-referenced in a teaching collection.

## Shared South Campus rooms

The materials are kept in the South Campus Clinic’s seminar area, where other departments also maintain displays. Hale’s catalogue records shelf labels, illustration titles and the intended academic section. It does not set screening criteria or address eligibility. The page describes the collection’s organization and subject area without offering health advice or making claims about services.
