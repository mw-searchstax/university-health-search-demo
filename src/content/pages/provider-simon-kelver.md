## Adjustable furniture display

Simon Kelver maintains a display of adjustable classroom furniture for Rehabilitation teaching. The exhibit pairs each object with a design drawing that labels its parts and shows how its configuration is represented on paper. A small index connects the display labels to a set of blank room plans.

## Design materials at the center

The collection is housed at the Rehabilitation Center beside mock apartment layouts used in accessibility discussions. Kelver keeps the drawings with the objects so visitors can compare the physical display with its annotated plan. These are classroom materials about design and environment, not assessments of a person’s needs. The linked service page gives broader context for the academic topics covered by the fictional unit.
