## Schematic image collection

Iris Bellan maintains a collection of schematic skin-layer illustrations for Dermatology teaching. Captions name the layers shown and identify the image format, such as a cross-section drawing or a labeled overview. A provenance card travels with each illustration, recording its fictional collection number and the teaching set where it appears.

## Shared seminar materials

The collection is displayed in the South Campus Clinic’s teaching annex, where seminar rooms also hold materials from other departments. Bellan keeps the captions and provenance cards together so that illustrations remain connected to their context. The images are educational diagrams, not patient photographs. This page describes how the fictional institution labels and organizes them; it does not interpret skin changes or offer medical guidance.
