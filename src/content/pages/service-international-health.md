## A regional reference collection

International Health is a fictional university unit that organizes regional maps, terminology cards and edition records for classroom use. Its reference desk groups materials by subject and publication details so students and faculty can compare how public-health topics are described across regions.

## Using the catalogue

Each catalogue entry identifies its source, edition and intended learning context. The collection supports classroom discussion of regional terminology and document organization; it does not provide care recommendations or represent an operating service.
