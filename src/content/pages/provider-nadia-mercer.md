## Heart diagram comparison

Nadia Mercer leads a classroom comparison of historical heart diagrams and modern schematic teaching models. The collection pairs each image with a date label, a caption and a note about the format used to represent the heart. Learners can compare how the illustrations are arranged and annotated across different teaching materials.

## Gallery and classroom

The display is housed at the fictional Heart Center, beside the Cardiology anatomy classroom. Mercer maintains the captions and the order of the diagrams so that the historical and schematic items remain easy to distinguish. The collection is educational and illustrative; it does not report research results or provide clinical explanations. A related provider profile focuses on a separate heart-diagram atlas, while this page describes the comparison exhibit.
