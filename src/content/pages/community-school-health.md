## Classroom reading kits

School Health Education describes fictional reading kits assembled by Alder Coast University Health for classroom discussion. Each kit has a topic card, a picture-label sheet and a short teacher note about the intended reading activity. The topics are arranged by age-appropriate vocabulary and document format, rather than by clinical instruction.

## Teacher-facing materials

The sample catalogue separates picture cards from teacher notes so that readers can see who each item is designed for. A card might name a classroom subject and identify the illustrations it contains; a teacher note describes how to compare captions or sort words into a glossary. The materials are invented and contain no personal stories or real school details.

The page connects the educational collection with the fictional pediatrics subject area and Children's Clinic location. It describes reading materials only and gives no health guidance, school schedule or service availability.
