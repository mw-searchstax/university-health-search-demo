## A distinct campus setting

The fictional Alder Coast Sleep Center occupies a low-rise building beside the university's academic wing. A covered walkway joins the two entrances. Inside, a small lobby opens onto an orientation room; a quieter corridor leads to demonstration bedrooms and an adjoining observation room.

The room layout gives students a concrete view of the setting described by the Sleep Medicine unit. Dimmable lighting, bedside equipment displays and a wall-mounted floor plan are part of the demonstration environment.
