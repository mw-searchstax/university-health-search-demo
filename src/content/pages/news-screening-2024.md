## A room reassigned in April 2024

On April 12, 2024, the fictional Alder Coast University reassigned a former seminar room in its library to the Community Screening reference collection. The room arrangement combined a shared reading table, a leaflet rack and a cabinet for earlier university notices.

The change brought together materials previously kept in separate departmental offices. Background reading and sample program leaflets were placed beside one another; dated notices remained in the archive cabinet.

## What the update recorded

The announcement documented a change in campus space and the organization of a reference collection. It did not announce a screening event or report clinical results.
