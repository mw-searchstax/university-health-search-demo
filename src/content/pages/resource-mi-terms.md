## Meaning of the term

Myocardial infarction refers to damage and death in part of the heart muscle after its blood supply is interrupted. The coronary arteries supply the muscle with blood and oxygen. A blockage can deprive part of the muscle of oxygen.

## A related term

Cardiac arrest means the heart suddenly and unexpectedly stops beating. It is a different event; the two terms aren't interchangeable.
