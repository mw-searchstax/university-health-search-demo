# Heart Center Collection Guide

Fictional demonstration website. Not a real healthcare provider.

## Three teaching exhibits

This collection guide describes three fictional exhibits arranged by the Alder Coast University Health teaching gallery. Each exhibit uses cardiology vocabulary as a subject label for a library-style display. The descriptions explain how the collection is organized; they do not give medical advice, describe a real service or report a clinical result.

## Exhibit A: anatomy atlas shelf

The atlas shelf holds reproductions of general anatomy plates beside a card listing author, edition and subject terms. The card groups entries under broad headings such as heart structure, circulation and historical illustration. These labels help learners practice browsing a reference shelf and comparing a subject heading with a book title.

## Exhibit B: instrument history case

The history case presents replicas of measurement instruments from different eras. Its timeline emphasizes changes in materials and design vocabulary, not diagnostic performance. A catalogue sheet records the display name, approximate period, material and exhibit section for each object. Every date and object description is part of the invented exhibit.

## Exhibit C: community poster archive

The poster archive places sample public-education graphics beside notes about typography, audience labels and publication context. Visitors can compare how the word cardiology appears in a subject line, a caption and an index entry. The posters contain no real contact information and make no recommendations about care.

## Collection organization

The gallery index groups these materials as an academic collection connected to the fictional Heart Center. The exhibits remain distinct: the atlas shelf is a print reference collection, the instrument case is a design-history display, and the poster archive is a communications collection. Their shared subject vocabulary makes them useful for a search exercise while preserving a clear purpose for each item.
